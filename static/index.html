🔬 Feature Engineering Pipeline

  • 1. Skills & Interests Binarization:

    Parsed semicolon-separated values into discrete multi-hot indicator vectors using MultiLabelBinarizer for 80 unique skills and 38 unique interests.

  • 2. Education Categorical Encoding:

    Transformed ordinal education levels (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD) into integer and one-hot embeddings via LabelEncoder.

  • 3. Age Standardization:

    Normalized candidate age feature distribution with zero mean and unit variance using StandardScaler.

  • 4. Hybrid Ranking Engine:

    Blends supervised Random Forest multi-class posterior probabilities (55%) with Cosine content overlap vectors (45%) to guarantee high relevance even on sparse or novel profiles.
